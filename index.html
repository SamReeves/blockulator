🚀 Create New Future

Predictable, constant rate. Value declines linearly from 100% to 0%.

🔄 Direct Transfer Tool

Transfer ownership of unlocked futures without the marketplace

📖 How It Works

1

Create

Deposit ETH, pick a distribution and lifetime. A contract is deployed holding your ETH.

2

Accumulate

Value accrues to the current owner according to the distribution curve over time.

3

Transfer

Transfer ownership to extract the accumulated payout. The remaining balance passes to the new owner.

4

Expire

At end of lifetime, the final owner can withdraw all remaining ETH.

⚙️ Technical Details

Distribution Types

  • Uniform: Constant rate
  • Gaussian: Bell curve, peak at midpoint
  • Exponential Decay: Front-loaded, 95% paid by 60% of lifetime
  • Exponential Growth: Back-loaded, 95% paid in final 40%
  • Linear Decay: Triangular, high at start
  • Inverted Gaussian: U-shaped, high at extremes
  • Linear Growth: Triangular, low at start

Payout Formula

Payout uses the distribution's probability density function:

payout = balance × ∫[pdf(t)] dt new_balance = balance - payout

On-Chain Math, Markets, and Games

Scientific computing and mechanism design, implemented entirely as smart contracts

🔬 What This Is

Blockulator is a collection of smart contracts that implement mathematical functions, financial instruments, and game mechanics directly on the Ethereum blockchain. Everything runs on-chain -- calculations, state, assets, payouts. No databases, no APIs, no off-chain dependencies.

🧮 What's Here

🔢 Math Library

30+ on-chain math contracts: trig, logarithms, exponentials, statistical distributions, combinatorics. Callable from other contracts or through the calculator interface.

📈 Futures

Time-weighted payout contracts with 7 distribution curves. Value accrues according to the chosen distribution; ownership transfers extract the accumulated payout.

🎖️ Pixel Badges

32x32 pixel art stored entirely on-chain. Draw, edit, and use as your on-chain identity. All image data lives in the contract.

🎮 Games

Nine autonomous game contracts exploring different mechanism designs: auctions, timers, coordination games, and adversarial dynamics.
