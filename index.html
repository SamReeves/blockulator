📖 How It Works

1

Deposit ETH, pick a distribution and lifetime. A contract is deployed holding your ETH.

2

Accumulate

Value accrues to the current owner according to the distribution curve over time.

3

Transfer

Transfer ownership to extract the accumulated payout. The remaining balance passes to the new owner.

4

Expire

At end of lifetime, the final owner can withdraw all remaining ETH.

⚙️ Technical Details

Distribution Types

  • Uniform: Constant rate
  • Gaussian: Bell curve, peak at midpoint
  • Exponential Decay: Front-loaded, 95% paid by 60% of lifetime
  • Exponential Growth: Back-loaded, 95% paid in final 40%
  • Linear Decay: Triangular, high at start
  • Linear Growth: Triangular, low at start

Payout Formula

Payout uses the distribution's probability density function:

payout = balance × ∫[pdf(t)] dt new_balance = balance - payout

FP127

Higher precision. Same 256-bit word.

A signed 127.128 fixed-point math library written in pure Huff assembly for the EVM.

The Problem

Dominant Ethereum community math libraries drop precision in dangerous ways.

What FP127 Does Differently

Full-Word Precision

We iterate until the full 256-bit word is full of correct information. The other guys just worry about the multiplication ceiling on uint128.

34 Operations

Arithmetic, transcendentals (exp, ln, log2, log10, sqrt, cbrt, pow), rounding (floor, ceil, round, frac), comparisons, utility functions, and special functions like Lambert W₀ and factorial -- operations the other libraries simply don't have.

Competitive Gas

Written in pure Huff assembly. Despite carrying twice the precision, FP127 is gas-competitive with Solidity-based libraries on many operations. Check the benchmarks for the full comparison.
