❓ How Gaussian Futures Work

The Hot Potato That Pays You

A Gaussian Future distributes its value over time following a bell curve. Passing the potato pays you, not the catcher!

📈 Distribution

🎯 How to Play

  1. Deploy: Create with ETH, set lifetime (hour/day/week)
  2. Wait: Value peaks at midpoint (bell curve)
  3. Transfer: Transfer ownership to claim your payout
  4. Repeat: New owner can transfer again
  5. Expire: Final owner claims remaining ETH

💡 Strategy

  • ✅ Transfer near midpoint for maximum value
  • ✅ Fully deterministic - calculate before transferring
  • ⚠️ Each transfer permanently reduces balance
  • ⚠️ Mind gas costs vs payouts
