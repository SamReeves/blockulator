📖 Understanding Gaussian Futures

🔥 The Hot Potato That Pays You

A Gaussian Future distributes its value over time following a bell curve. Unlike a regular hot potato, passing it pays YOU, not the catcher!

🎯 How to Play

1
Deploy Create with ETH, set lifetime (hour/day/week/month/year)
2
Wait Value peaks at midpoint following the bell curve
3
Transfer Transfer ownership to claim your calculated payout
4
Repeat New owner can transfer again until contract expires
5
Expire Final owner claims all remaining ETH after expiry
